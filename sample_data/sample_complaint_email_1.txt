Subject: Complaint - Discoloration observed in Metformin HCl 500mg tablets, Batch MFH2405B

Dear Quality Team,

We are writing to formally report a quality complaint regarding a recent shipment received
from your Unit-II facility.

Product: Metformin Hydrochloride Tablets IP 500mg
Batch / Lot No.: MFH2405B
Manufacturing Date: 12-Jan-2025
Expiry Date: 11-Jan-2027
Quantity Affected: 4,500 tablets (approx. 45 strips out of the 500-strip consignment)

During routine incoming inspection at our warehouse in Ahmedabad on 2nd July 2026, our QC
team observed yellowish-brown discoloration on approximately 10-12% of tablets inspected from
strips in cartons #14 through #19. The discoloration appears concentrated on one face of the
tablet and is not present on tablets from other batches received in the same shipment.

Unaffected tablets from the same batch, and tablets from batch MFH2403A received last month,
show no such discoloration. Storage conditions at our facility have been maintained per label
claim (below 25°C, protected from light) since receipt, so we do not believe this is a
transit or storage-related issue on our end.

Given this is an oral solid dosage product close to a 2-year shelf life claim, we are
concerned about a possible stability or excipient interaction issue and are requesting an
urgent investigation. We have quarantined the remaining stock from this batch and await your
guidance on further disposition.

Please treat this as a high priority matter given the patient safety implications of tablet
discoloration in a chronic-use diabetes medication.
