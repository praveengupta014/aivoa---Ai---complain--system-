Subject: URGENT - Foreign particulate matter found in API drum, Paracetamol API Batch PCM-A2231

Hello,

This is an urgent complaint from Vertex Biologics Japan regarding an Active Pharmaceutical
Ingredient (API) shipment received on 28-Jun-2026 via ocean freight.

Product: Paracetamol API, Pharmacopoeial Grade (USP/EP)
Batch/Lot Number: PCM-A2231
Manufacturing Date: 03-Mar-2026
Expiry/Retest Date: 02-Mar-2028
Quantity Affected: 2 drums out of 20 (approx. 50 kg)

Upon opening drum #7 and drum #14 during sampling for our incoming raw material QC, our
analysts observed dark fibrous particulate matter embedded within the white crystalline
powder. Visual inspection under white light confirmed foreign material clearly distinguishable
from the API itself, estimated at 8-10 visible fragments per drum, ranging 1-3mm in size.

The remaining 18 drums from the same batch, sampled per our standard AQL plan, did not show
this contamination on visual inspection, though we have not yet completed full testing.

This is a critical finding for an API intended for finished dosage form manufacturing, as
foreign particulate contamination poses direct patient safety risk and would require full
batch rejection if confirmed across the lot. We have placed the entire batch PCM-A2231 on
hold and request your immediate root cause investigation, including a review of your drum
filling/closing operations and warehouse environmental controls at the time of packing.

Please respond within 48 hours given the severity of this finding.
